Introduction

Go Be Love

Go Be Love is a purpose-driven digital magazine and apparel brand rooted in the streetwear and casual fashion market. Founded on the belief that everyday purchases can create meaningful social impact, Go Be Love targets college students and young adults who want their style choices to stand for something. The brand pairs high-quality, gender-neutral hoodie designs with a transparent philanthropic mission: a percentage of every sale directly funds children's education at Xhope Children's School in Uganda. The magazine serves as the editorial heart of the brand — publishing stories about the students in Uganda whose lives are changed, spotlights on socially conscious designers, community-driven fashion culture, and the intersection of style and purpose. Go Be Love exists to close the gap between commerce and compassion, transforming the act of getting dressed into a daily opportunity to give back.
